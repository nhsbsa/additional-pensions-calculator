Your additional pension

Paying for your additional pension

You can buy additional pension with a single one-off payment or by regular monthly payments.

Compare a single one-off payment and monthly repayments

Understand the differences between these options.

Single one-off payment

Buying your additional pension with a single one-off payment.

You should pay the one-off payment to your employer, and they will make the payment to us.

You will pay slightly less with a single one-off payment

If you buy additional pension with a single one-off payment, you will pay slightly less than if you pay with regular monthly payments.

This is because a single one-off payment is calculated based on only your age.

Regular monthly payments

Buying your additional pension with regular monthly payments deducted from your pay.

With regular monthly payments:

  • you will pay over an agreed period from 1 year to 20 years
  • you will usually pay the same amount each month
  • payments will start from the date your employer receives your agreement
  • your employer will not take deductions from your pay until we confirm acceptance in writing
  • your first deduction will be more than 1 month because your employer will backdate it

Following reviews of the Scheme, the cost of future instalments of an existing arrangement could be changed.

You will pay slightly more with monthly payments

If you buy additional pension with regular monthly payments, you will pay slightly more than if you pay with a single one-off payment.

This is because the cost is based on your normal pension age and you are paying by instalments over a longer period of time compared to a single one-off payment.
